Privacy Policy

Who helps us

We share data only with the services that run the Bureau, each bound by a data processing agreement:

  • Stripe: checkout, payments, tax and receipts. For some purchases Stripe acts as merchant of record and is then a controller in its own right (Stripe privacy policy).
  • Resend: sends our emails (EU sending region).
  • Zoho: hosts our mailbox, hello@manifestationbureau.com (EU data centre).
  • GitHub: hosts this website and runs our automated order processing. The files it keeps contain order numbers and dates only, no names, wishes or emails.

Some of these companies are based in the US. Transfers are covered by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the EU's Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long we keep it

  • Payment and order records: as long as Latvian accounting law requires (up to 5 years), in Stripe.
  • The video and certificate files: not kept. They're made, emailed to you and deleted.
  • Emails with us: deleted within 24 months unless we need them for an open issue.

This website

This site sets no cookies and uses no analytics or trackers. Fonts are served from our own domain. Our host, GitHub, logs IP addresses for security. When you continue to checkout, you're on Stripe's page and Stripe's cookie policy applies.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, or ask us to correct it, delete it, restrict its use or hand it over in a portable format. You can also object to processing based on legitimate interest. Email hello@manifestationbureau.com and we'll answer within one month. If you're a recipient and want your name removed from our records, write to us too.

You can complain to the Latvian Data State Inspectorate (dvi.gov.lv) or the data protection authority where you live.
